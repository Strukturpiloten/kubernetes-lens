# Rust source guidance

The [root guidance](../AGENTS.md) remains authoritative. Follow the
[foundation API](../docs/foundation-api.md), [architecture](../docs/architecture.md), and
[native acceptance evidence](../docs/fixtures/native-acceptance.md). Preserve native source evidence,
privacy and bounded processing; keep existing independent suites and cleanup contracts. KubernetesLens
must remain independent of BoxFerry product crates. Source controls alone do not establish native
qualification or release delivery.
